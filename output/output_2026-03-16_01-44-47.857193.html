Imagine starting your first job with the confidence to speak up, contribute meaningfully, and be taken seriously without pretending to be an extrovert. Many introverted students feel pressured to "act outgoing" to succeed, but real professional presence comes from leveraging your authentic strengths.

This 2-hour workshop helps introverts build confidence, visibility, and influence in a way that feels natural.

Through guided reflection and interactive activities, students will learn how to:

  • Speak up in group settings without feeling drained
  • Communicate confidently during interviews and presentations
  • Build long-term professional visibility through authentic strategies

Participants will also be introduced to the QUIET Framework™, a practical model that transforms introverted tendencies into workplace advantages.

Who Should Attend:
Introverted students or anyone who feels overlooked, overwhelmed, or less confident in high-interaction settings especially before internships, interviews, or early career transitions.

Participants will leave with:

  • A clearer understanding of the strengths introverts bring to the workplace
  • Three practical techniques to project presence without overexertion
  • Greater confidence advocating for their preferred work style

Benefits of Attending:

  • Career readiness: Navigate meetings, networking, and reviews with confidence
  • Self-advocacy: Learn to be seen and valued without changing your personality
  • Reduced stress: Understand how influence and visibility work, minus the anxiety

This is a workshop in collaboration with NUSOne.

Speaker(s)

Dean Shams

Dean Shams helps quiet professionals build executive presence, communicate with impact, and be noticed without being loud.

With more than 15 years of experience as a communication strategist, Dean has seen that career success isn’t about talking more but about making your ideas heard with clarity and confidence. He draws on neuroscience and practical communication tools to show participants how to speak up in interviews, meetings, and group settings while staying authentic.

Dean is the founder of Nabyula Consulting & Training, author of Speak Smart, Make Your Mark, and creator of the Q.U.I.E.T. Framework™, a system that turns introverted tendencies into career advantages. His workshops give participants practical strategies they can apply immediately to be noticed, valued, and trusted in the workplace.

Tell Your Story: Discover Identity, Purpose, and Life Opportunities Date & Time: Wednesday, 25 March 2026, 3.00pm to 5:00pm
Venue: NUS Central Library Level 4 Multipurpose Room

This interactive 2-hour workshop invites students to step back from grades, résumés, and expectations to reflect on a deeper question: Who am I becoming, and how do I want to show up in the world? Through guided storytelling exercises, students explore formative experiences—challenges, failures, and turning points—and uncover the values, strengths, and motivations that shape their unique identity.

Designed for university students across multiple disciplines, the workshop blends self-reflection with practical application. Students learn how to translate personal insights into stories they can confidently use in interviews, networking conversations, leadership roles, and everyday decision-making. Multiple practice sessions help participants move from self-discovery to clear, authentic expression.


Only 40 slots available! Registration is on a first-come, first-served basis.

About the Speaker, Ms Lu Liu
Lu Liu is the Co-Founder and Special Advisor of JustCo, a Singapore-based coworking company. As a founding member of the leadership team, she played a key role in building JustCo from the ground up, with a strong focus on people, culture, and service excellence.

With over 20 years of experience as a mentor, educator, and business leader, Lu has worked with learners across a wide range of ages and backgrounds.

​She has volunteered extensively as a mentor and tutor, and is currently mentoring founders through the Young Business Leaders Network.

Today, Lu spends much of her time advising teams, helping them deliver meaningful and memorable experiences for customers. She is a strong believer that leadership, culture, and service are powerful drivers of long-term business success, and she enjoys sharing these lessons with students and young professionals.

​Prior to JustCo, Lu worked at Procter & Gamble across the US, Europe, and Asia, as well as Ernst & Young in New York. She holds an MBA from Columbia Business School and a Bachelor of Science from NYU Stern, and is fluent in English and Mandarin.

SCS AI Conference 2026  

Leading with AI Fluency  

 

Date: Friday, 10 April 2026,

Time: 9.00am - 2.00pm (Registration starts at 8.30am)

Venue: Mandarin Oriental, 5 Raffles Avenue, Singapore 039797

A unique opportunity for your students to engage with Singapore's AI transformation journey. In an AI-forward reality, organisations and professionals across and beyond the tech industry must be ready to lead digital transformation at scale. Building on Singapore's national call to cultivate ""bilingual"" AI talent, the inaugural SCS AI Conference 2026 highlights the growing importance of AI fluency at every stage of readiness. This is a valuable chance for your students to join industry leaders, decision makers, and experts as we shape the conversation on building a vibrant, AI-enabled economy - giving them insights into their future careers in this exciting field!

 

Highlights of Conference

  • Two keynote sessions - Hear from leaders driving AI and business transformation

  • Student-Focused Track on ""Future Talents: The AI Journey Ahead"" featuring
    • Fireside Chat on ""AI and Your Career: What's Next for Young Talent""
    • Speed Networking Sessions with tech industry partners
