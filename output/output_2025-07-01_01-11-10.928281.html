Singapore-China Youth Forum 2025 2025-07-19 2025-07-19 N/A 🔗 IOs, NGOs, Non-Profits & Social Services

All Faculty/ Schools

Warm greetings from Business China.

We are pleased to invite you to attend the Singapore-China Youth Forum 2025, taking place on 19 July 2025, Saturday, at the Singapore Polytechnic Convention Centre.

Jointly organised by Business China and Wanhe International, with the support of the China Education Association for International Exchange (CEAIE), the forum is held in conjunction with the 35th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Singapore and China. This meaningful occasion provides a timely platform for young people from both countries to come together in open dialogue and collaborative exchange.

The forum will bring together 1,000 youth participants—500 from Singapore and 500 from China—for a full-day programme focused on exploring regional opportunities, strengthening mutual understanding, and cultivating cross-cultural friendships. We welcome students from universities and polytechnics to take part in a day of discussion and discovery, where diverse perspectives will converge to shape ideas for the future.

With the theme “Together for a Common Future”, the programme will include keynote addresses by distinguished leaders, panel discussions featuring experts and youth voices, as well as smaller group sessions that encourage participants to engage in meaningful conversations. These interactions will not only help participants build a deeper appreciation of the Singapore-China relationship but also allow them to expand their horizons through international exchange.

Early bird registration ends on 18 May 2025.

Global Aviation and Maritime Symposium 2025 2025-07-14 2025-07-14 N/A 🔗 Education

All Faculty/ Schools

 All students interested in the maritime sector

The Singapore Ministry of Transport (MOT) will be organising the Global Aviation and
Maritime Symposium (GAMS) on Monday, 14 July 2025, at the Fairmont Ballroom, Raffles City Convention Centre.

The GAMS is a high-level by invitation-only event that aims to gather Transport Ministers, senior officials, and key industry leaders from the aviation and maritime sectors to exchange views and share perspectives on current and future challenges. The Secretaries-General of the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) and International Maritime Organization (IMO) will be attending the Symposium and will hold an exclusive in-person dialogue with the participants.

There will be a High-Level Panel with the theme ""Navigating Global Transport in a Changing World: Challenges, Opportunities, and Cooperation"", as well as a Ministerial & Industry Roundtable with the theme of ""Shaping Tomorrow’s Transport: People, Technology, and Policy"".
