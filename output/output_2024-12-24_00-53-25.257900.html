"What would happen if we studied what was right with people versus what's wrong with people?"
- Donald O. Clifton, American Psychologist and father of Strengths-based Psychology

We have been told that our weaknesses present great opportunities for development, but recent research suggests that focusing on our strengths instead yields better results than trying to improve on our lack. At this workshop, you will learn how your strengths can play out in everyday life and how to tap on them during your internship stints and/or job roles.

The activities in this workshop are designed to help you:

  • Leverage your understanding of your strengths to evaluate the extent of suitability of your work environment and/or job role
  • Use your strengths to brand yourself on LinkedIn and at interviews
  • Practically exercise your strengths in workplace scenarios to enhance professional effectiveness and relationships

Come and ignite your awareness of your strengths and begin your journey to becoming uniquely effective wherever you go!

Compulsory Requirements:
After registering on TalentConnect, it is compulsory to complete the Teams Form or your registration will be disqualified. Provide up to three reasons/motivations for completing the strengths profiling assessment and attending the workshop. In the event of oversubscription, selection will be based on the reasons/motivations provided.

Pre-Work:
Prior to the workshop, students will complete the Clifton Strengths Assessment to uncover their Top 5 strengths. It is strongly recommended that you read through your personalised report and bring it to the workshop. Do note that the Clifton Strengths Assessment (U.P. S$30.00) and workshop are fully subsidised. However, failure to turn up for the workshop will result in a penalty of S$30.00.

Do you aspire for a career in consulting? Then join us in this workshop to master case interviews, a common assessment tool adopted by consultancy firms. The workshop will be led by case interview expert Daniel Sia, a former Bain Consultant in Southeast Asia and Australia.

This workshop will familiarise you with key strategies for approaching case interviews, case structuring foundations, case math, and provide you with a chance to practise a full case in a group and receive valuable feedback from the facilitator.

The session will be concluded with a brief tour of CaseCoach, during which we will discuss how to efficiently and effectively utilise the platform to prepare for case interviews. All NUS students can register for complimentary access to the CaseCoach platform.

Through this workshop, you will have:

  • An introduction to case interviews, including where it is used, why it is used, format, competencies assessed
  • A full live case practise as a group, start to finish
  • A deep dive into case structuring, including what is structuring, what a good structure looks like: the AIM test, key academic frameworks
  • Interactive case structuring practise with 4-5+ case questions and coach feedback
  • A deep dive into case math with interactive practise, including 2-3+ math questions and coach feedback
  • Insights on how to prepare for success, including what a well-prepared candidate looks like and how to use the CaseCoach platform to prepare

Requirements:

Ahead of the session, students should log onto CaseCoach and familiarize themselves with the platform. We recommend watching the 5-minute Management Consulting Recruitment Process video and downloading the Case Interview Scorecard.

If you have not, register now for complimentary access to CaseCoach (not applicable to NUS Business School students) where you can practise with other students, take preparatory courses and watch real case interviews. 

Who should attend?

This session is targeted at students who have some experience with case interviews. It is advised that participants have practised a minimum of 10 cases before attending this workshop to maximise their learning.
