Learning Journey with Stroke Support Station 2025-11-25 2025-11-25 N/A 🔗 IOs, NGOs, Non-Profits & Social Services

All Faculty/ Schools

 all student

About Our Learning Journey Programme

Empact is working on youth development programmes that create meaningful career opportunities and professional development experiences for young people in Singapore. We are currently running a programme under the National Youth Council (NYC)'s Discovery On My Way platform, featuring an exclusive Learning Journey with Stroke Support Station (S3).

This in-person learning journey, held at Enabling Village, is designed to provide students with direct exposure to the disability and healthcare sector. The session features:

  1. An introductory tour of S3's Centre for Stroke Recovery and introduction to their mission and impact story; 
  2. Immersive hands-on workshop where students participate in a creative art-making session alongside stroke survivors (no art skills needed - just bring an open mind!); and
  3. Career insights fireside chat with professionals from across clinical, organisational development (HR or IT) and corporate (Partnerships, Communications and Media) functions, sharing real career journeys and insight into working in the disability sector
  4. A tour of the Enabling Village, to learn how the disability sector ecosystem operates in Singapore

Programme Details:

  • Date: 25th November 2025, Tuesday
  • Time: 10:00am - 12:30pm
  • Location: Enabling Village (S3 Centre and Empact office)
  • Target: Students aged 17-25, interested in social impact and healthcare sector careers

This programme exposes you to meaningful career opportunities in the social and healthcare sector, offering them professional insights and practical experience regardless of their field of study. Students will discover diverse roles spanning clinical, programmes, HR, IT, and more.

    Discover+ AEROSPACE | As one of the world’s leading aviation hubs, Singapore is soaring at the forefront of the global aerospace industry. From cutting-edge aircraft maintenance, repair and overhaul (MRO) and advanced avionics, to precision manufacturing, air traffic management, and aerospace R&D — the sector is a dynamic engine of innovation and growth.

    Anchored by world-class facilities such as Seletar Aerospace Park and Changi Airport, and powered by global giants like ST Engineering, Rolls-Royce, Pratt & Whitney, and Boeing, Singapore’s aerospace ecosystem continues to expand its reach across the skies.

As the next era of flight takes shape, the demand for bold, skilled professionals has never been greater. The future of aerospace is being built today — and it’s taking off from Singapore.
