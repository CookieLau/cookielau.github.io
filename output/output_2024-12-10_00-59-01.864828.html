Dear students,

 

Apply to join McKinsey flagship youth leadership program- Young Leaders for Inclusion (YL4I) in Singapore!

 

What is YL4I Singapore: a ~12 week program where teams of young leaders (like yourselves!), together with McKinsey coaches, work with non-profits to drive social impact by solving specific problem statements. The program will be structured in a field-and-forum approach:

  • Fieldwork (weekly): work in sprints within your teams, supported by McKinsey coaches with problem-solving templates
  • Forums (monthly): joint learning sessions and sprint reviews

 

What is the timeline and commitment required: from end-September 2024 to end-January 2025 (including a 4-week EOY break in December), spending a few hours a week on progressing the problem statement

Designed to provide insights into the rapidly evolving sustainability efforts in the healthcare industry, this session will offer participants valuable insights into the unique position of healthcare toward the climate crisis, the innovative solutions being pioneered to create more sustainable hospitals, as well as the diverse opportunities open to management associates to contribute towards the sustainability of the sector.
