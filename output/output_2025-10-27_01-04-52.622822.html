 All undergraduates

Maritime & Port Authority of Singapore’s Global Internship Award (MPA GIA) is a talent development programme targeting local, high-achieving undergraduate students in their penultimate year from both maritime and non-maritime disciplines in universities. 

The programme aims to raise awareness of the importance of the maritime industry to Singapore and the global economy, as well as increase their understanding of the enriching and rewarding maritime career opportunities available.

This involves a 12-week programme with maritime companies from May to October and includes a stint at any of their overseas offices for up to 6 weeks.

MPA GIA will fully cover the allowance, the costs of a return economy-class airfare, overseas accommodation, travel and travel-related expenses (e.g. insurance).  For the programme in Singapore and overseas, students will undergo structured experiential learning with the host companies and gain hands-on industry exposure to deepen their industry knowledge.

About the Speaker, Mr Jack Sim
Also known as ""Mr Toilet,"" Jack is a Singaporean social entrepreneur and philanthropist who has become globally recognized for his pioneering efforts in sanitation advocacy and public health.

""From a poor kid who failed at school to a global game-changer, Jack Sim used one simple thing, gumption, to spark a revolution."" (excerpt from the book he authored)

Key achievements:

  • Author of “The Gumption of Mr Toilet, Mobilizing the World to Effect Change (published in 2024) and Simple Jack (published in 2011)
  • Founder of the Restroom Association of Singapore, the World Toilet Organization (WTO), and the World Toilet Day initiative, now officially recognised by the United Nations on November 19.
  • Founder of the BoP (Bottom of the Pyramid) Hub and continues to create social businesses and projects aiming to lift the global poor out of poverty.
  • His work has broken down taboos around sanitation, mobilized international support, and turned toilets into a global conversation topic.
  • He has received numerous accolades, including being named a Schwab Foundation Social Entrepreneur of the Year, an Ashoka Global Fellow, and one of Time Magazine’s Heroes of the Environment

Established in November, 2020, New Oriental Global Education and Training Division (GET) evolved from Overseas Testing Department, a core department of New Oriental Education and Technology Group, which originated from Beijing New Oriental School founded in 1993.

GET provides both language courses covering international English proficiency tests and academic courses including IB, AP, A-Level, international school admission preparation and competition, as well as improving learner's global competency.

Dubai Business Associates (DBA) Programme
Location: Dubai, UAE
Duration: 9 months (September – June)

The Dubai Business Associates (DBA) programme is a fully funded, prestigious graduate management training programme designed for high-potential recent graduates and young professionals. Hosted under the patronage of the Executive Office of His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, the programme offers a unique blend of business training, consulting projects, and cultural immersion in Dubai.

Participants undergo intensive consulting and leadership training, work on real-world projects with leading Dubai-based organisations, and engage in global networking opportunities. The programme also provides mentorship, professional development workshops, and exposure to the dynamic business ecosystem of Dubai.

Who Should Apply?

  • Recent graduates or early-career professionals with a strong academic background.
  • Individuals with leadership potential, problem-solving skills, and an interest in global business.
  • Those looking to gain hands-on experience in consulting, strategy, and business development.

Why Join?

  • Fully funded opportunity (including flights, accommodation, and stipend).
  • Work on strategic projects with top organisations in Dubai.
  • Gain world-class training from industry leaders and consulting firms.
  • Build a global network of peers and mentors.
