Debonding-On-Demand, Sustainability Challenge:

Adhesives are crucial in modern manufacturing, enabling fast assembly, bonding of different materials, and lightweight construction. However, they can hinder the repair and recycling of bonded parts.

Adhesives that can be debonded on demand could revolutionize the circular economy by making products easier to dismantle and recycle.

Despite numerous attempts, there are still few effective debondable adhesives available on the market.

The Challenge:

We seek innovative, out-of-the-box approaches to achieve one of the following two targets for debonding:

Target 1: We are interested in new chemical strategies for debonding on demand that:

  • fit to existing technologies for chemically curing adhesives (Polyurethane, Epoxy, Silicone, Acrylate)
  • act on the backbone of the chemical network via reversible or cleavable covalent bonds.
  • are economically attractive

Please think beyond well described approaches, such as Diels-Alder based systems, that have shown not to be technically and commercially viable.

 

Target 2: We are looking for application proposals where debonding on demand can be beneficial in terms of recycling, repair or rework, for instance by

  • meeting regulatory requirements
  • recovery of high value material
  • minimizing waste
  • enhancing product lifetime.

 

Idea submission: Each submission should cover one or more of the following key points:

  • Brief overview of the Idea (One-Pager) description of the technical concept
  • Technical and commercial benefits and limitations of the solution
  • Optional: Flowsheets, drawings, examples, or references for the proposed solution or for comparable systems
  • Optional: Potential collaboration concept for development, scaling up, testing and implementation

 

Assessment criteria: The evaluation criteria are defined as follows

For new technical debonding approaches

  1. Compatibility with existing adhesive technologies
  2. Economical attractiveness, cost and availability of materials as well as manufacturing effort
  3. Suitability of the debonding trigger for typical adhesive applications
  4. Novelty: already published?

 

For new debonding applications

  1. Moderate lifespan of the product with the bonded parts
  2. Regulatory pressure
  3. Value (economical or emotional) of bonded parts
  4. Accessibility of the adhesives joint
  5. Suitable trigger: is the adhesive accessible by a specific trigger

 

Timeline for idea submission: June 30th, 2025 

Announcement of the winning concepts: 6 weeks after submission deadline.

 

Target groups: Everyone is welcome to participate. The intended target groups include students, PhD candidates, postdoctoral researchers, research and development institutions, start-ups in the fields of organic chemistry, polymer chemistry, economics, chemical economics, as well as professionals in chemistry, biology, and engineering.

Competition Background

The 10th edition of the ""Envision the Future"" BOE Global Campus Innovation Challenge is hosted by BOE Technology Group Co., Ltd. and guided by the LCD Branch of the China Optics and Optoelectronics Manufacturers Association. This competition is a global platform for technological innovation, open to university students around the world.

Since its launch in 2015, the BOE Global Campus Innovation Challenge has marked a decade of progress. It has attracted over 2,000 teams from more than 300 universities worldwide, covering cutting-edge fields such as display technology, the Internet of Things, and smart medical engineering.

The competition aims to promote deep integration among industry, academia, and research, empower the transformation of youth innovation, and showcase the technical ingenuity and commercial potential of the next generation at the forefront of science and technology.

We encourage university students to participate by forming teams of 2 to 5 members. The competition centres around BOE’s semiconductor display business, expanding through its integrated “1+4+N + Ecosystem” model—encompassing MLED, sensor technologies and solutions, IoT innovation, and smart healthcare engineering.

Participants are invited to develop innovative products, apply frontier technologies, and propose original solutions rooted in real-world application scenarios. The goal is to reimagine the future—and transform everyday life through technology.

Competition Format

Participating teams from each region will present their projects in the form of a project proposal. These submissions will be evaluated and scored online by a panel of expert judges. Based on the evaluation results, winners of the preliminary round will be announced along with the list of finalists advancing to the Global Finals.

The Global Finals will take place at BOE Technology Group’s headquarters in Beijing, where final awards will be determined through a combination of live presentations and final defence scores. The most outstanding teams will be crowned as winners.

Competition Themes

This year’s competition introduces a selection of industry-driven themes that align closely with BOE’s “1+4+N + Ecosystem” business structure. Participants may freely choose one topic to develop their submission.

Focus Areas

  • Semiconductor Display Technologies
  • MLED Technologies
  • Sensor Technologies and Solutions
  • Internet of Things (IoT) Innovation
  • Smart Healthcare Engineering

Eligibility Requirements

  1. Who can participate:
    • Current undergraduate, master's, or doctoral students (including international students) from any accredited university.
  2. Team composition:
    • Each team must consist of 2 to 5 members.
  3. Team flexibility:
    • There are no restrictions on academic year, major, or institution—teams may be formed across different schools and disciplines.
  4. Submission criteria:
    • Participants must complete all required steps within the designated timeframe:
      registration, track selection, team formation, and project proposal submission. Only submissions that meet these criteria will be considered valid.
  5. Additional note:
    • Teams are encouraged to submit their registration early. The project proposal can be uploaded later once finalized.

*Please pay attention to the submission deadline!

Competition Timeline

  • Project Proposal Submission
    May 8 – June 30, 2025
    Teams must formally register and submit their project proposals during this period.
  • Preliminary Evaluation
    Mid-July 2025
    Submitted projects will undergo expert review and scoring.
  • Announcement of Global Finalists

    Finalist teams advancing to the global round will be publicly announced.
  • Global Finals & Roadshow
    Late July to August 2025
    Finalist teams will participate in presentations and on-site defense sessions at BOE Headquarters in Beijing.

Note: The timeline is subject to change. Please check the official website regularly for updates.
