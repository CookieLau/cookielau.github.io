Have you just received a job offer and want to ensure the employment contract is fair? Or are you actively job hunting and unsure of what to look for in a potential offer?

Join us in this workshop, a collaboration between NUS Centre for Future-ready Graduates and Young NTUC, the youth wing of the National Trades Union Congress (NTUC), to equip yourself with essential knowledge on employment contracts and workplace rights.

This workshop will help you develop the knowledge and skills to make well-informed decisions about your career, ensuring you are equipped to navigate the complexities of employment contracts.

Key Takeaways:

  • Understand what an employment contract is and why it is essential for protecting your career.
  • Learn about Part IV of the Employment Act and how it safeguards your rights as an employee
  • Familiarise yourself with key terms and conditions typically found in employment contracts
  • Gain clarity on post-termination restrictions and garden leave clauses
  • Learn how to identify breaches of contract and the steps to take in response
  • Understand what constitutes wrongful dismissal and how to address workplace harassment

Why attend?

Gain the confidence to evaluate your job offer critically and ensure you understand your rights in the workplace.

Thinking about pursuing a PhD degree but not quite sure if you are equipped with the skills for the journey?

Embarking on a PhD candidature is a significant commitment, and the motivation behind it should be aligned with one’s career aspirations. Apart from the training to acquire research skills through original research, it is important to understand the soft skills and mindset needed to succeed during and beyond the PhD candidature.

Join us for a panel discussion to get a glimpse of what a PhD journey entails and what is needed to thrive throughout your graduate school experience.

In this session, you will:

  • Understand the difference between a PhD candidature and a typical postgraduate coursework programme
  • Learn how to prepare for a successful PhD journey, such as having the motivation, passion, mental resilience, along with the importance of building a strong support network
  • Learn how to identify opportunities to acquire career-relevant soft skills during the PhD candidature beyond your research topic or domain expertise

Congratulations! You have been offered an internship or a job. Now what?

After receiving a job offer, you will have to evaluate and manage the offer while considering various factors and possible scenarios. You will need to know your motivators, as well as the things to look out for in an employment contract and remuneration package. The next step is to accept or reject the offer professionally and ethically and apply appropriate negotiation techniques to achieve the best package.

In this interactive workshop, expert trainer Clement Ong will guide you in navigating the complexities of managing job offers and salary negotiation through case studies, small group discussions and facilitated reflection. This session will include live discussions in the main room and breakout rooms, where you will work on a case study, respond with your solutions and will be given live expert feedback.

In this workshop, you will learn to:

  • Identify your motivators and effectively evaluate employment contracts and remuneration packages
  • Professionally and ethically handle multiple job offers, mastering the essential Dos and Don’ts
  • Learn the Dos and Don’ts in salary negotiation and develop effective responses to salary-related inquiries
  • Understand different types of negotiation tips and techniques
  • Learn the art of gracefully accepting or declining an offer in a professional manner
  • Cultivate the ability to handle rejections with grace and professionalism

Who should attend?

  • Students who are currently applying and receiving offers for internships or full-time roles
