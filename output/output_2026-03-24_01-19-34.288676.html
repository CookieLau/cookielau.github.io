NUSOne x CFG Workshop: Tell Your Story: Discover Identity, Purpose, and Life Opportunities 2026-03-25 2026-03-25 N/A 🔗 Education

All Faculty/ Schools

 All undergraduate students

Tell Your Story: Discover Identity, Purpose, and Life Opportunities Date & Time: Wednesday, 25 March 2026, 3.00pm to 5:00pm
Venue: NUS Central Library Level 4 Multipurpose Room

This interactive 2-hour workshop invites students to step back from grades, résumés, and expectations to reflect on a deeper question: Who am I becoming, and how do I want to show up in the world? Through guided storytelling exercises, students explore formative experiences—challenges, failures, and turning points—and uncover the values, strengths, and motivations that shape their unique identity.

Designed for university students across multiple disciplines, the workshop blends self-reflection with practical application. Students learn how to translate personal insights into stories they can confidently use in interviews, networking conversations, leadership roles, and everyday decision-making. Multiple practice sessions help participants move from self-discovery to clear, authentic expression.


Only 40 slots available! Registration is on a first-come, first-served basis.

About the Speaker, Ms Lu Liu
Lu Liu is the Co-Founder and Special Advisor of JustCo, a Singapore-based coworking company. As a founding member of the leadership team, she played a key role in building JustCo from the ground up, with a strong focus on people, culture, and service excellence.

With over 20 years of experience as a mentor, educator, and business leader, Lu has worked with learners across a wide range of ages and backgrounds.

​She has volunteered extensively as a mentor and tutor, and is currently mentoring founders through the Young Business Leaders Network.

Today, Lu spends much of her time advising teams, helping them deliver meaningful and memorable experiences for customers. She is a strong believer that leadership, culture, and service are powerful drivers of long-term business success, and she enjoys sharing these lessons with students and young professionals.

​Prior to JustCo, Lu worked at Procter & Gamble across the US, Europe, and Asia, as well as Ernst & Young in New York. She holds an MBA from Columbia Business School and a Bachelor of Science from NYU Stern, and is fluent in English and Mandarin.

In partnership with NUSOne
