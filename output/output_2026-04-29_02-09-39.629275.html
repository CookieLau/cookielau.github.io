Human Intelligence in the Age of AI 2026-05-07 2026-05-07 N/A 🔗 Information Communications Technology

All Faculty/ Schools

School of Computing

Overview

Moving Beyond the Hype

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly becoming part of everyday work. AI is now accessible to nearly everyone. But when everyone has access to the same technology, what actually creates advantage? Not more tools. It is Human Intelligence.

The ability to think critically, interpret information, communicate meaningfully, and make decisions in complex situations will define how effectively AI is used.

This event explores the evolving relationship between human capability and artificial intelligence, bringing together perspectives from academia, industry, and emerging talent.

We explore a more important question: How can humans work with AI more intelligently?

Why This Conversation Matters

AI adoption is accelerating across industries. Yet many organizations and individuals are still figuring out how to integrate these tools meaningfully into real work.

Understanding technology alone is not enough.

What Makes This Event Different

This is not a purely technical discussion about AI tools.

We’ll focus on the human dimension of AI adoption, bringing together educators, industry practitioners, and students to exchange perspectives on how AI is shaping work, learning, and leadership.

Who Should Attend

  • Professionals navigating AI in their industry

  • Students exploring future-ready skills

  • Educators shaping the next generation

  • Entrepreneurs and innovators interested in human-centered technology

Agenda

Fireside Chat

  • “Human Integration with AI: A Smarter Way to Work Every Day”

  • Interact with HI AI Experts, Educators, Industry Practitioners, and Student Leaders.

Human Intelligence (HI) Workshop

  • Get hands-on interactive activity that facilitate your thinking and link back to the fireside chat.

Networking Dinner

  • Mingling and Networking over dinner.

Human Intelligence in the Age of AI is a fireside chat and interactive workshop exploring how humans can work with AI more intelligently in everyday life and work. As AI becomes more accessible, the real differentiator is no longer technology alone, but the human abilities that shape how it is used - critical thinking, judgment, creativity, communication, and ethics.

Bringing together perspectives from academia, industry, and emerging talent, this event offers a human-centered conversation on the future of work, learning, and leadership in an AI-driven world.

2026 Bloomberg Academy 2026-07-27 2026-07-31 N/A 🔗 Information Communications Technology

All Faculty/ Schools

 All undergraduate students

Program Description: 
Bloomberg Academy in Singapore is an early insights program designed for students currently in university with a non-finance or limited finance background, graduating between Dec 2027 and Aug 2028, who aspire to build a career in Bloomberg’s Analytics & Sales program.  

 
Throughout the program, you will be immersed in a varied array of topics, people, and activities. From understanding financial market players to learning the functionality of the Bloomberg Terminal, you’ll learn directly from our industry experts and discover how to apply this knowledge in a fast-paced, client-focused environment. You’ll also have the opportunity to connect with recent Analytics & Sales Program Graduates, hear about their experiences, and learn from our Recruiters on how to prepare for an interview opportunity at Bloomberg. 

 

As you explore our inclusive culture and career opportunities, you’ll have the chance to qualify for early interviews for the 2027 Analytics & Sales Summer Internship program in Singapore. Successful completion of the Bloomberg Academy culminates in an Interview Day on the final day of the week.   

Program Dates: 27th to 31st July 2026 

(Inclusive of Interview Day on 31st July 2026) 

 

 

What’s in it for you? 

  • Enhance your understanding of Bloomberg, and the Analytics and Sales roles 
  • Network, job shadow, and learn about a ‘Day in the Life’ of our Analytics and Sales representatives 
  • Introduction to Financial Markets and interactive training on the Bloomberg Terminal 
  • Hands-on experience with presentation and mock interviews 
  • Learn about our culture of Philanthropy and participate in volunteering work 
  • Connect with a community of like-minded peers and expand your professional network 
  • Gain practical tips and insights from our Recruiters to prepare for the Superday 
  • Access to Bloomberg Market Concepts (BMC), a 10-hour self-paced course that provides a visual introduction to the financial markets: Economics, Currencies, Fixed Income and Equities — woven together from Bloomberg data, news, analytics and television  

 

You’ll need to be/have: 

  • Available to join us in-person for the full duration of the program (27th to 31st July, 2026) 
  • Graduating from all studies between Dec 2027 and Aug 2028, and available for a 10-week internship between mid-May and end-July 2027.  
  • Legal authorization to work full-time in Singapore and will not require visa sponsorship now or in the future 
  • Demonstrable interest in financial markets and the aspiration for a future career in Bloomberg’s Analytics & Sales Program 
  • Ambition to pursue a client-facing career, with a strong interest in relationship management and sales 
  • Ability to communicate effectively and articulate thought process clearly  
  • Resilience to learn and adapt quickly in a dynamic fast-paced environment 
  • Business proficiency and fluency in English (written and verbal) 
  • Students from non-Finance degree disciplines are highly encouraged to apply; our aim is to have a varied intake of candidates 

 

 We'd love to see:  

  • Demonstrated interest in and/or exposure to the practical application of Python 

 

Application Process for Academy: 

  1. CV Screening 
  2. Pre-recorded Video Interview 
  3. Phone Interview 

 
 

Application Deadline:  
Applications must be submitted online by 10th May 2026, early applications are strongly encouraged!
