NUS Biomedical Sciences Interdisciplinary Industry Academy Webinar Series 2026-10-07 2026-10-07 N/A 🔗 Education

From Mechanistic Bench Science to Global Regulation: Advancing Animal-Free Safety Assessment via New Approach Methodologies (NAMs)As global regulations evolve, the cosmetic industry has acted as a pioneer in eliminating animal testing. The transition toward animal-free safety assessment represents one of the most significant shifts in modern biomedical
and industrial science.

This session explores the interdisciplinary journey and the technological transition of
replacing traditional in vivo toxicology—specifically the Draize test for skin and eye irritation assessment—with robust, science-based New Approach Methodologies (NAMs). Dr. Alépée will discuss the engineering and biological characterization of 3D Reconstructed Human Epidermis (RhE) models and their integration into sophisticated testing toolboxes. The presentation will detail the evolution of Integrated Approaches to Testing and Assessment (IATA) and Defined Approaches (DA), illustrating how the rule-based integration of physico-chemical properties and in vitro data can provide higher predictive accuracy than legacy animal models. Through case studies on liquids, solid chemicals, and surfactants, this talk demonstrates the successful translation of academic research into standardized OECD Test Guidelines, ensuring consumer safety through the lens of the 3Rs and Next-Generation Risk Assessment
(NGRA) frameworks.

Dr. Nathalie Alépée is a distinguished expert in molecular biology and investigative toxicology with nearly 30 years of experience across the pharmaceutical and cosmetic sectors. Following her PhD research at CNRS and a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Cambridge, she spent a decade at Pfizer before joining L’Oréal R&I in 2007. Her career is defined by her role as a scientific bridge-builder, translating advanced mechanistic research into validated New Approach Methodologies (NAMs). With an H-index of 35 and over 3,300 citations, Dr. Alépée has authored approximately 100 peer reviewed papers and has been instrumental in the international adoption of over 15 OECD Test Guidelines (including TG 431, 439, and 492B). As the French Representative at the OECD and a key contributor to the ICCS and EPAA, she leads
interdisciplinary efforts to implement animal-free safety assessments globally.

From powering industries to feeding populations, commodities play a vital role in the global economy. Yet, despite its impact and breadth, the commodities sector remains relatively unfamiliar to many students. Beyond trading, the industry offers diverse career opportunities across analytics, operations, technology, supply chain, risk management, and other commercial functions.

Join us for this introductory industry session to gain a deeper understanding of the commodities ecosystem and explore the exciting career pathways available within the sector. Through insights from industry professionals across the oil and agricultural commodities markets, you will learn how global commodity markets operate, the key functions that keep international trade moving, and what it takes to build a successful career in the industry.

The session will feature speakers from the oil and agri-commodities sectors who will share their experiences, industry perspectives, and career journeys, providing students with a practical understanding of this dynamic and fast-paced field.

Key topics include:

  • The fundamentals of commodity trading and how global commodity markets function
  • An introduction to oil trading, including the market intelligence and insights that inform trading decisions (courtesy of Sparta Commodities)
  • An overview of agricultural commodity trading, with a focus on grains and wheat markets
  • Day-to-day responsibilities within trading and related commercial roles
  • Career opportunities across trading, analytics, operations, supply chain, technology, risk management, and support functions
  • The skills, mindsets, and attributes employers look for in aspiring commodities professionals

The session will conclude with a panel discussion and Q&A, offering participants the opportunity to engage directly with industry practitioners and gain practical advice on entering and thriving in the commodities sector.

Who should attend:

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate students interested in learning more about commodities, global trade, and financial markets

Today’s workplaces are increasingly diverse, bringing together individuals with different cultural backgrounds, identities, perspectives, and communication styles. The ability to work effectively across differences is a key professional skill that enables stronger collaboration, innovation, and workplace success.

This interactive workshop will help participants develop the awareness, mindset, and practical skills needed to navigate diverse work environments with confidence and professionalism.   • Build greater cultural awareness and adaptability in multicultural work environments

Calling all students! Want to make your communication skills more visible, observable, and employability-relevant?

Join CommCircle.
A small group peer practice circle where you can try, refine, and build confidence in workplace communication in a supportive, low-stakes environment. What to expect

In each session, trained CommPeers (top performers from CommsLab, CFG1600/S) will facilitate practice and provide structured, actionable feedback to help you develop, demonstrate, and articulate communication competencies that employers value.

You can work on areas such as:

  • Speaking and interpersonal communication
  • Influencing and persuasion
  • Stakeholder communication
  • Structured thinking and clear articulation
  • Executive presence
  • Managing communication anxiety in high-stakes situations

What you need to prepare

To benefit fully from the session, please come prepared with a piece of communication you want to practise and get feedback on. You have flexibility in what you bring. Examples include:

  • Career and employer pitches (based on real job descriptions)
  • Stakeholder presentations
  • Internship reflections
  • Influencing without authority

Sessions run every Wednesday of even weeks.

Calling all students! Want to make your communication skills more visible, observable, and employability-relevant?

Join CommCircle.
A small group peer practice circle where you can try, refine, and build confidence in workplace communication in a supportive, low-stakes environment. You’re welcome to experiment, make mistakes, and learn through practice and feedback. CommCircle enables students to develop and demonstrate communication competencies valued by employers through practice, feedback, and real-world communication scenarios.

What to expect

In each session, trained CommPeers (top performers from CommsLab, CFG1600/S) will facilitate practice and provide structured, actionable feedback to help you develop, demonstrate, and articulate communication competencies that employers value.

You can work on areas such as:

  • Speaking and interpersonal communication
  • Influencing and persuasion
  • Stakeholder communication
  • Structured thinking and clear articulation
  • Executive presence
  • Managing communication anxiety in high-stakes situations

What you need to prepare

To benefit fully from the session, please come prepared with a piece of communication you want to practise and get feedback on. You have flexibility in what you bring. Examples include:

  • Career and employer pitches (based on real job descriptions)
  • Stakeholder presentations
  • Internship reflections
  • Influencing without authority

Sessions run every Wednesday of even weeks.

Registration

Sign up via this MSForm. Slots are first-come, first-served, and limited to keep group sizes small and feedback high quality. If you’re allocated a slot, CommPeers will send you a confirmation email with the session details.
