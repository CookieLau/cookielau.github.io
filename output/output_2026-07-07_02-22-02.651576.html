Program Description: 
Bloomberg Academy in Singapore is an early insights program designed for students currently in university with a non-finance or limited finance background, graduating between Dec 2027 and Aug 2028, who aspire to build a career in Bloomberg’s Analytics & Sales program.  

 
Throughout the program, you will be immersed in a varied array of topics, people, and activities. From understanding financial market players to learning the functionality of the Bloomberg Terminal, you’ll learn directly from our industry experts and discover how to apply this knowledge in a fast-paced, client-focused environment. You’ll also have the opportunity to connect with recent Analytics & Sales Program Graduates, hear about their experiences, and learn from our Recruiters on how to prepare for an interview opportunity at Bloomberg. 

 

As you explore our inclusive culture and career opportunities, you’ll have the chance to qualify for early interviews for the 2027 Analytics & Sales Summer Internship program in Singapore. Successful completion of the Bloomberg Academy culminates in an Interview Day on the final day of the week.   

Program Dates: 27th to 31st July 2026 

(Inclusive of Interview Day on 31st July 2026) 

 

 

What’s in it for you? 

  • Enhance your understanding of Bloomberg, and the Analytics and Sales roles 
  • Network, job shadow, and learn about a ‘Day in the Life’ of our Analytics and Sales representatives 
  • Introduction to Financial Markets and interactive training on the Bloomberg Terminal 
  • Hands-on experience with presentation and mock interviews 
  • Learn about our culture of Philanthropy and participate in volunteering work 
  • Connect with a community of like-minded peers and expand your professional network 
  • Gain practical tips and insights from our Recruiters to prepare for the Superday 
  • Access to Bloomberg Market Concepts (BMC), a 10-hour self-paced course that provides a visual introduction to the financial markets: Economics, Currencies, Fixed Income and Equities — woven together from Bloomberg data, news, analytics and television  

 

You’ll need to be/have: 

  • Available to join us in-person for the full duration of the program (27th to 31st July, 2026) 
  • Graduating from all studies between Dec 2027 and Aug 2028, and available for a 10-week internship between mid-May and end-July 2027.  
  • Legal authorization to work full-time in Singapore and will not require visa sponsorship now or in the future 
  • Demonstrable interest in financial markets and the aspiration for a future career in Bloomberg’s Analytics & Sales Program 
  • Ambition to pursue a client-facing career, with a strong interest in relationship management and sales 
  • Ability to communicate effectively and articulate thought process clearly  
  • Resilience to learn and adapt quickly in a dynamic fast-paced environment 
  • Business proficiency and fluency in English (written and verbal) 
  • Students from non-Finance degree disciplines are highly encouraged to apply; our aim is to have a varied intake of candidates

 all students

The role of industrial toxicologists has evolved significantly, reflecting advances in toxicology and the changing needs of industry. This evolution is expected to continue, with increasing emphasis on next-generation testing paradigms that may establish new standards for safety assessment.

This presentation highlights key themes - including current priorities, technological advancements, collaboration and innovation, and the value of resilience and a positive mindset - in demonstrating how industrial toxicologists can deliver meaningful, value-added impact.

This webinar series showcases real-world case studies from industry projects to help students understand current challenges and how they are addressed in practice. By highlighting industry practices, decision-making processes, and the application of both technical expertise and professional competencies, the series aims to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry expectations.

About the Speaker:

Dr. Sang-Tae Kim earned his PhD in Organic Physical Chemistry from Arizona State University and completed an NIH postdoctoral fellowship at the University of North Carolina. He is a Senior Principal Toxicologist and leads Global Toxicology and Product Safety at Ashland Specialty Chemical Company. He is a Diplomate of the American Board of Toxicology (DABT), European Registered Toxicologist (ERT), Fellow of the Academy of Toxicological Sciences (ATS), and United Kingdom Registered Toxicologist (UKRT).

Over the past 30+ years, he has led human and environmental risk assessments and supported regulatory compliance with chemical products, active (and non-active) pharmaceutical ingredients, and medical devices across multinational consumer products and biotechnology/pharmaceutical organizations, with more than 200 scientific publications.

He has served as an ad hoc peer reviewer for the U.S. EPA Science Advisory Committee on Chemicals (SACC) and currently serves on the Society of Chemical Manufacturers and Affiliates (SOCMA) Environmental Committee and Health & Safety Committee, the International Pharmaceutical Excipients Council–Americas (IPEC-Americas) Scientific Affairs Committee, and the American Chemistry Council (ACC) Chemical Management & Science Policy Committee

NUS Biomedical Sciences Interdisciplinary Industry Academy Webinar Series 2026-10-07 2026-10-07 N/A 🔗 Education

 all students

From Mechanistic Bench Science to Global Regulation: Advancing Animal-Free Safety Assessment via New Approach Methodologies (NAMs)As global regulations evolve, the cosmetic industry has acted as a pioneer in eliminating animal testing. The transition toward animal-free safety assessment represents one of the most significant shifts in modern biomedical
and industrial science.

This session explores the interdisciplinary journey and the technological transition of
replacing traditional in vivo toxicology—specifically the Draize test for skin and eye irritation assessment—with robust, science-based New Approach Methodologies (NAMs). Dr. Alépée will discuss the engineering and biological characterization of 3D Reconstructed Human Epidermis (RhE) models and their integration into sophisticated testing toolboxes. The presentation will detail the evolution of Integrated Approaches to Testing and Assessment (IATA) and Defined Approaches (DA), illustrating how the rule-based integration of physico-chemical properties and in vitro data can provide higher predictive accuracy than legacy animal models. Through case studies on liquids, solid chemicals, and surfactants, this talk demonstrates the successful translation of academic research into standardized OECD Test Guidelines, ensuring consumer safety through the lens of the 3Rs and Next-Generation Risk Assessment
(NGRA) frameworks.

Dr. Nathalie Alépée is a distinguished expert in molecular biology and investigative toxicology with nearly 30 years of experience across the pharmaceutical and cosmetic sectors. Following her PhD research at CNRS and a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Cambridge, she spent a decade at Pfizer before joining L’Oréal R&I in 2007. Her career is defined by her role as a scientific bridge-builder, translating advanced mechanistic research into validated New Approach Methodologies (NAMs). With an H-index of 35 and over 3,300 citations, Dr. Alépée has authored approximately 100 peer reviewed papers and has been instrumental in the international adoption of over 15 OECD Test Guidelines (including TG 431, 439, and 492B).
